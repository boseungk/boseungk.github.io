WEBSITE PRIVACY

Website privacy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

About your visit

This notice covers the MyAnimalRoom product website and public feedback board. There is no sign-up or payment. We use no first-party analytics or advertising scripts. Reading product pages or suggestions does not create a visitor identification cookie. Fonts and artwork are hosted with the website; social content is linked rather than embedded.

Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process connection information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

When you contribute, your optional nickname, message and date are public. Keep private information out of posts. A random browser token in the API-only __Host-feedback cookie lasts up to 90 days and helps limit duplicate votes and repeated posts. Only its hash is stored by the server. Clearing cookies or using another device creates a separate identity; votes are not verified unique people. Suggestions, comments and votes are retained while the board operates. Expired token hashes are cleared during maintenance without removing past contributions.

The board uses Cloudflare Workers, D1 and, on first contribution, Turnstile. Cloudflare may process connection and browser information for security. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. The app does not send your journals, tasks, focus history or backup account to the board. Email the public post link to request moderation or deletion. Anonymous authorship cannot always be verified or recovered.

If you email us, we receive your email address, the name you include and your message so we can respond to your enquiry. Contact contact@myanimalroom.com with questions or requests concerning your enquiry. External social platforms have their own policies. The mobile app is in development and will have separate privacy information when it becomes available.
