A FEW MORE THINGS

Get to know us.

Is the app available yet?

MyAnimalRoom is in development for iOS and Android and has not launched yet. We will share release news here and on our official channels.

Are these screens from the app?

These demonstrations use artwork and development screens from the app. They illustrate focusing, completion and memories; no records are saved here. The final interface and features may change before launch.

When does my animal friend appear?

Your chosen companion joins you when you start focusing. After you finish and confirm, a time-of-day mark is added to your memories and you return to your space.
