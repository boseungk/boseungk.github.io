A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A few things you might be wondering.

Can I download the app now?

The iOS and Android app is in development and has not launched. Release updates will be shared on this website and our official channels.

How are the app and YouTube channel connected?

MyAnimalRoomLife is our animation channel, using the same characters and world as the app. We are building an experience where the friends you meet on screen also have a place in your room.

Can I use the app on this website?

This is our product and updates website. The animated companions and room are previews of artwork from the app in development. Focus and journal features are planned for the mobile app.
