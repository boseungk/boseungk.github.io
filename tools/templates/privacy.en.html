Website privacy

This notice explains how information is handled on the My Animal Room website and feature suggestion board.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Browsing the website

You can browse without signing up or paying. We use no advertising tracking scripts. We count only daily totals of how visitors arrived (for example Instagram or search), page views and app store button taps. This count uses no cookies and stores no IP address or anything that tells visitors apart. The way you arrived is kept briefly in the browser’s session storage for that tab and added to the app store links, so store statistics can show which channel led to a download. Reading pages or suggestions does not create a participation cookie.

Fonts and artwork are served by this website. Social videos and feeds are linked rather than embedded.

Posting suggestions and comments

Your optional nickname, message and posting date are public. If you leave the nickname blank, your post is shown as anonymous. Keep contact details and sensitive information out of public posts; use email for personal enquiries.

We use posting and voting records and a random participation identifier to operate the board and limit duplicate votes and repeated submissions. A cookie is stored in your browser when you contribute and lasts for up to 90 days.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. This may limit posting or voting, or prevent us from connecting your previous participation. Participation does not require an account, so vote counts do not represent identity-verified individuals.

Retention and removal requests

Information needed to operate the board and recognise participation is retained while the board operates. To request that a post or comment be hidden or removed, email its link and your request to contact@myanimalroom.com. We will review and handle the request.

Because participation is anonymous, verifying authorship or recovering previous participation may be limited.

Service providers and security

We use GitHub to serve the website and Cloudflare to operate the board and prevent automated submissions. These providers may process connection information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to provide and secure their services.

See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

Email enquiries

When you email us, we receive your email address, any name included in the message and your enquiry. We use them to reply and follow up on your request. You can also email contact@myanimalroom.com to ask about or request removal of information in your enquiry.

External channels and the mobile app

Links to YouTube, Threads, Instagram and other external services are subject to those services’ privacy policies.

The app does not send your journals, tasks, focus history or backup account to this board. See the App Privacy Policy for information handled by the mobile app.
